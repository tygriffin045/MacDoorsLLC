Services

The work a garage door actually needs.

Separate pages for new doors and repairs, because those are different jobs. This is the full list.

New door installation

Single, double, and custom openings. Old door removed. Tracks, springs, and seals set for the new weight.

Opener installation

Belt-drive for bedrooms over the garage, wall-mount when headroom is tight, Wi-Fi remotes if you want them. Safety eyes tested with a 2×4 on the floor.

Spring and cable replacement

Torsion and extension systems. Pair replacement only. Door rebalanced so you can lift it with one hand at mid-travel.

Off-track and panel repair

Reset, reinforce, or replace the damaged section. We say when a repair is throwing money at a door that should be replaced.

Tune-up

Hardware tightened, rollers checked, hinges lubed with garage-door lubricant — not WD-40 — and the reverse tested.

Commercial, by request

Light commercial sectional doors if that work is requested.
